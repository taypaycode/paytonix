> **Purpose:** Replace the current narrow Revenue Data Integrity homepage funnel with a broader Paytonix positioning that better reflects the actual scope of work: analytics engineering, MarTech systems integration, AI/LLM production workflows, campaign automation, CRM/warehouse architecture, and executive reporting.

> **Primary positioning:** Paytonix is a technical systems studio for revenue teams.
>
> **Core promise:** Paytonix architects and builds the systems connecting marketing, CRM, sales, data, automation, AI, and reporting.

**The Problem**

## H2

# The dashboard is only the last mile.

## Copy

A modern revenue system spans ads, websites, analytics, enrichment, CRM, lifecycle messaging, sales workflows, warehouses, AI tools, and executive reporting.

Each platform may work perfectly on its own.

The failures happen between them.

Customer identities fragment. Campaign context disappears. CRM objects drift from warehouse models. Automation logic lives in one-off scripts. AI workflows stop at a chat window instead of writing safely into production systems. Analysts repair the same reporting problems repeatedly. Operators manually coordinate steps that should be deterministic.

**The result is a stack full of powerful software that still depends on human glue.**

When that happens:

- acquisition teams optimize against incomplete outcomes
- RevOps spends time reconciling systems instead of improving them
- analysts rebuild logic that should be reusable
- sales and marketing teams inherit inconsistent CRM state
- executives see polished dashboards without confidence in the machinery behind them
- technical operators become the permanent integration layer between platforms

# 15. How Paytonix works

## Reuse current "How It Works" numbered-step component

Change the sequence from:

`Trace → Test → Quantify → Repair → Monitor`

to:

`Assess → Architect → Build → Automate → Monitor`

---

## 01 — Assess

We identify the systems, workflows, bottlenecks, failure modes, and business outcomes that matter.

This may involve tracing a customer journey, reviewing CRM architecture, examining a warehouse model, auditing an automation workflow, or scoping an AI system.

---

## 02 — Architect

We define the system boundaries, canonical entities, data contracts, integrations, control points, and operating model.

The goal is not to add more tools.

It is to decide how the tools should work together.

---

## 03 — Build

We implement the technical foundation.

Depending on the engagement, that may include:

- APIs
- pipelines
- SQL models
- CRM integrations
- LLM workflows
- dashboards
- webhooks
- orchestration
- validation
- data contracts
- automation

---

## 04 — Automate

We remove repetitive operational work where the process is understood well enough to encode safely.

That may include targeting, enrichment, campaign creation, list management, CRM writeback, lifecycle actions, reporting, QA, and sync logic.

---

## 05 — Monitor

Where appropriate, we add controls for:

- failures
- freshness
- schema drift
- identity coverage
- duplicated records
- workflow execution
- revenue reconciliation
- attribution integrity
- downstream state
